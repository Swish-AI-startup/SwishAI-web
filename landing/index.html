Technical check

A standard video is enough.

  • MP4 or MOV in H.264/HEVC, plus selected AVI files.
  • Video duration depends on the plan: 1 minute, 5 minutes or the complete session.
  • Use a computer when you want the full annotated video; mobile returns the session data.

Good to know

Questions before your first shot.

Do I need an account to try SwishAI?

No. You can analyze the first minute of a video without signing up. Create a free account only when you want to start saving sessions.

Where is my video uploaded?

Nowhere. The analysis runs locally in your browser and the original video stays on your device.

What do I need to record a session?

A phone, a stable support or tripod, one clearly framed hoop and enough space to keep the shooter and ball path visible.

Does SwishAI work on a phone?

On mobile you receive shot counts and statistics. Use a computer when you also want the complete annotated video.

Can I correct a shot if the analysis gets it wrong?

Yes. You can manually correct detected events before keeping the final session results.

What does Pro add?

Complete-video analysis, unlimited history, progress trends, in-session stamina, records, exports and the option to remove the SwishAI logo from videos.
