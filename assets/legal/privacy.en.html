Your video never leaves your device

This is the most important thing to know, so it comes first. SwishAI analyses your video locally: in your browser on the web, and natively in the app on desktop. There is no upload endpoint. We have no copy of your footage, we cannot look at it, and we could not hand it over if we were asked to.

What reaches our servers is limited to your account, your subscription, and a short numeric summary of each run. Everything below is about that.

Who is responsible

The data controller is SwishAI. For anything about your data, write to swishai23x@gmail.com.

What we collect, and why

Account

Your email address, a username if you choose one, and a hash of your password (never the password itself). If you sign in with Google or Apple we store the provider name and the account identifier that provider gives us, and no password at all. We also record whether your email is confirmed and when the account was created.

Purpose: running your account. Legal basis: performance of our contract with you.

Processing statistics

When a signed-in user finishes processing a video we store one row: the number of shot attempts, the number of baskets, the time it was processed, and an opaque digest that identifies that run. The digest is a one-way hash. It is not the file name, and the file name is deliberately not sent, so nothing about your video or your camera roll is revealed by it.

Purpose: showing you your own history and progress. Legal basis: performance of our contract.

Subscription

Your Stripe customer and subscription identifiers, your plan, its status, when the current period ends, and whether it is set to cancel. Billing address and tax identification are collected and held by Stripe at checkout, not by us. We never see or store card numbers.

Purpose: providing and billing the subscription. Legal basis: performance of our contract, and our legal obligation to keep tax records.

Email tokens

When you confirm an email address or reset a password we store a hash of the token we sent you, its expiry, and whether it has been used. The token itself only exists in the email.

Purpose: security of those flows. Legal basis: performance of our contract and our legitimate interest in preventing abuse.

Analytics

Google Analytics, and only if you agree. Nothing is loaded and no identifier is written before you accept: the tag is injected after you opt in, and if you decline we set Google's own opt-out flag. You can change your mind at any time through Cookie settings in the footer. Your choice is remembered locally under the key swishai.analytics-consent.

Purpose: understanding which parts of the app get used. Legal basis: your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

Server logs

Our API records ordinary request information, including IP address, for security and debugging. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in keeping the service working and secure.

What we do not collect

  • Your videos, and any frame or still image from them.
  • Card numbers.
  • Location data.
  • Contacts, photos library, or anything else on your device beyond the file you pick.

Who else processes your data

  • Stripe for payments, subscriptions and tax. Stripe acts as an independent controller for some of this and holds your billing details.
  • Google (Gmail SMTP) to deliver confirmation and password-reset emails.
  • Google Analytics, only after you consent.
  • Contabo GmbH (Germany), which hosts our API and its database on a virtual private server.
  • GitHub Pages, which serves the web app itself.

Some of these may process data outside the EEA. Where that happens, transfers rely on the safeguards those providers offer, such as the European Commission's standard contractual clauses or an adequacy decision.

How long we keep it

There is no time limit, and nothing is deleted on a schedule. Your account, your processing statistics and your subscription record stay for as long as the account exists, however old they get. That is deliberate: the point of the statistics is comparing how you are shooting today against how you were shooting months ago, and a run we had quietly deleted is a comparison you could no longer make. Deleting your account is what removes them.

Email and reset tokens expire within hours and are cleared. Analytics data follows Google Analytics' own retention settings. Stripe keeps invoices for as long as tax law requires, independently of us, which is why payment records survive account deletion.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account yourself from your profile. Doing so cancels any active subscription first, then removes your user record along with your statistics, your linked social sign-ins, your subscription row and any outstanding tokens. If the subscription cannot be cancelled the deletion is refused rather than leaving you billed for an account that no longer exists, and we will tell you so you can try again.

Your rights

You can ask us to give you a copy of your data, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to how we use it, and receive it in a portable form. Where we rely on consent you can withdraw it at any time. Write to swishai23x@gmail.com and we will respond within one month.

If you think we have handled your data badly you can complain to the Italian supervisory authority, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali, or to the authority where you live.

Automated decisions

The detection model analyses your video, but it makes no decision about you: it produces counts you read. There is no profiling and no automated decision-making with legal or similarly significant effects.

Children

Accounts are for people aged 14 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from younger children. If you believe a child under 14 has an account, tell us and we will remove it.

Changes

If we change this notice materially we will tell you by email or in the app before the change takes effect.

Language

This notice is written in English and translated for convenience. If a translation and the English version disagree, the English version governs.
