In short

  • Your videos never leave your device. SwishAI analyses them in your browser. There is no upload: we have no copy of your footage, we cannot look at it and we could not hand it over if asked.
  • What reaches us is your account, the numbers of your training sessions (shots, baskets and when they happened in the video) and, if you choose, your public profile.
  • Your profile is private by default. Nothing is public unless you make it so.
  • We do not sell your data and we show no advertising.

1. Who is responsible

The data controller is SwishAI. For anything about your data, write to swishai23x@gmail.com.

2. What we collect, why, and on what legal basis

Your account

Email address, nickname (and when you last changed it), country, date of birth, a hash of your password (never the password itself), whether your email is confirmed, when the account was created and when you accepted the terms. If you sign in with Google we store the identifier Google gives us and your email: never your name, and no password. We also keep the sign-in tokens that keep you logged in: they expire after 30 days without use, and changing or resetting your password ends every open session.

Why: to run your account. Legal basis: performance of our contract with you (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)).

We need your date of birth to check the minimum age and to switch on the 16+ features by themselves on your 16th birthday. It never appears on your profile and nobody else sees it. Legal basis: performance of our contract and our legitimate interest in protecting younger users (Art. 6(1)(f)).

Your training sessions

When you save a result we store: the session (day, name you give it, time zone); for each video, when it was analysed, how much of it was analysed and how long the file is, the version of the analysis, and every detected shot as a moment in the video (for example "at 0:42, made") together with any correction you make. We also store:

  • a file fingerprint: a one-way hash computed on your device from the file size, its first and last megabyte and the video's internal index (the part that describes its duration and tracks). The video cannot be rebuilt from it. We use it to warn you when you analyse the same video twice and to keep duplicates out of the leaderboards;
  • the recording date read from the video's own metadata, when there is one, so that a weekly leaderboard only counts videos filmed that week.

We never receive the file name, frames, images, sound or location of the video.

Why: to show you your diary, your progress and your records, and to keep leaderboards fair. Legal basis: performance of our contract; for duplicate and fairness checks, also our legitimate interest in fair leaderboards (Art. 6(1)(f)).

Manual corrections

You can correct what the model counted. We measure how much and in which direction results are corrected. Past a threshold, corrections are paused for 30 days and the corrected sessions do not count in the leaderboards. Corrected sessions show a "corrected by hand" label on your public profile.

Why: so that leaderboards compare real shooting. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in fair leaderboards. This check only affects leaderboards and corrections; if you think it got it wrong, write to us and a person will look at it.

Public profile, leaderboards and following (only if you choose)

If you make your profile public (possible from age 16), anyone with the link /u/your-nickname, even without an account, can see: your nickname, photo, country flag and the month you joined; how many activities you have, how many people follow you and how many you follow, with their lists; this week's numbers, the chart of the last 8 weeks, your records with their date, the days you trained in the last 6 months with the shots and baskets of each day, your totals and your most recent sessions (day, name, shots, baskets, percentage, the "corrected by hand" label). Never times, videos or the shot-by-shot detail. Public profiles appear in the leaderboards and in the search for registered users. Who you follow is stored so we can show you their results.

Your public profile also shows your SwishAI points and your position on the points leaderboard, and the description you choose to give your sessions. If you are on the Pro plan, a Pro sign appears on your photo and next to your nickname wherever your public profile shows up (profile, leaderboards, search, follower lists), and your profile shows the month you became Pro. A public profile may appear among the suggested players in other registered users' search, with the reason (for example a player you both follow, your country or this week's leaderboard). To award points we keep their movements (reason, points, date) for as long as you have an account.

With a private profile (which never shows whether you are Pro), other people see your nickname, a lock, any visible profile photo (see below) and how many activities, followers and following you have. Registered users can find your nickname in the search and send you a follow request. You choose: if you accept, that person sees your profile as described above and finds you in their "Following" leaderboard; nobody else does, and you never appear in the world leaderboard. You can remove a follower at any time, and they are not notified. If you decline, the person is not told and can ask again after 7 days. We store requests, acceptances and declines for this purpose. We may also show you, privately, the position you would have.

Why: the social features you asked for. Legal basis: performance of our contract; you can switch back to private at any time.

In-app notifications

We let you know inside the app, with the bell, when someone asks to follow you, starts following you or accepts your request, and when you beat one of your records. We keep each notification for 90 days. We do not send push notifications or emails for this.

Why: the social features you use. Legal basis: performance of our contract.

Profile photo

If you upload a profile photo, we store the cropped image until you remove it or delete your account. We remove metadata such as GPS location when preparing the picture. From age 16 it appears wherever we show your nickname, including on a private profile. If you are 14 or 15, only the followers you have accepted can see it. We can remove a reported picture or hide it when we hide a profile.

Reports and moderation

If you report a profile we store who reported whom, the reason and any note. When we moderate (hiding a profile, freeing a nickname) we record the action. After a nickname change the old one stays reserved to you for 30 days.

Why: to keep the community safe. Legal basis: our legitimate interest and our legal obligations.

Subscription and Pro codes

If you subscribe: your Stripe customer and subscription identifiers, plan, status and renewal date. Billing address and tax ID are collected and kept by Stripe, never by us; we never see card numbers. If you redeem a Pro code we record that you redeemed it and when.

Why: to provide and bill Pro. Legal basis: performance of our contract and our legal obligation to keep tax records (Art. 6(1)(c)).

Comments you send us

"Send us a comment" stores your message, its category and, if you attach it, a technical summary of your last analysis (video length, resolution, format, engine, shots and baskets). If you are signed in, it is linked to your account.

Why: to fix problems and improve the app. Legal basis: our legitimate interest.

Error reports from the app

When something goes wrong (the app fails, an analysis stops or resumes, a request to the server does not go through) the app sends us an anonymous technical report: app version, page, browser and system type, whether the app is installed, screen width, available memory and, for an analysis, the video's size, duration, resolution and format and where it stopped. Both in the database and in the server logs we keep only a reduced version, without your account, IP address, file name or free text; in the database for 30 days.

Why: to find and fix crashes. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in a working app.

Server logs

Like any web service, our server records technical information about requests, which can include the IP address. We use it only for security and diagnosis. The logs overwrite themselves once they exceed 50 MB in total.

Legal basis: our legitimate interest in a secure, working service.

Analytics (only with your consent)

Google Analytics, only if you accept it. Nothing is loaded before you do. You can change your choice at any time from Usage statistics in Settings and at the bottom of the home page. If you withdraw your consent, Google Analytics stops collecting data at once; the cookies it had already stored (_ga) stay in your browser until they expire or you delete them. Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)), which you can withdraw at any time.

3. What stays on your device

The app keeps some data in your browser's storage, and it never reaches us: the sign-in tokens and your account's basic details (email and nickname), your language and consent choices, your recent searches, your last result with the name of the analysed file, drafts and changes not yet sent, so you do not lose them if the page reloads. The last result can include the video itself, kept locally for at most 48 hours. If the app stops unexpectedly it also keeps a short technical note, which it sends as an error report when it reopens. Signing out or deleting your account removes tokens, results, videos and drafts from the device; clearing the site's data in your browser removes everything.

4. What we do not collect

  • Your videos, frames, images or sound from them.
  • File names, your photo library or contacts.
  • Your location.
  • Card numbers.

5. Who else processes data for us

  • Contabo GmbH (Germany): the server that runs our API and database, located in France.
  • GitHub (GitHub Pages, USA): serves the web app's files. It sees ordinary connection data such as your IP address.
  • Google: Google sign-in if you use it; Gmail to send confirmation and password emails; Google Analytics only with your consent.
  • Stripe: payments, subscriptions and tax, when payments are active. For billing data Stripe is an independent controller.
  • Telegram: receives the daily backup of our database as a password-protected archive.

Some of these providers are based in or may process data outside the European Economic Area (notably the USA and, for Telegram, other countries). Where that happens the transfer relies on an adequacy decision (such as the EU–US Data Privacy Framework, for certified providers) or on the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

6. How long we keep data

  • Account, sessions, corrections and social data: as long as your account exists. The diary is meant to compare you today with months ago, so we do not delete it on a schedule.
  • On the Free plan older sessions are hidden, not deleted: you see them again with Pro.
  • Error reports: 30 days. Email confirmation links: valid for 24 hours; password reset links: 1 hour. For both we keep only a one-way fingerprint, for as long as the account exists.
  • Server logs: they overwrite themselves past 50 MB.
  • In-app notifications: 90 days.
  • Backups: deleted data can survive in the backups for the limited time each backup is kept.
  • Stripe keeps invoices for as long as tax law requires, independently of us.

7. Deleting your account

You can delete your account yourself from your account settings. If you have a paid subscription, you first cancel it (so that you are never billed for an account that no longer exists). Deletion removes your account, sessions, videos and fingerprints, shots and corrections, follows in both directions, reports you made and received, comments, notifications, sign-in links and tokens, subscription row and moderation history. A Pro code you redeemed stays marked as used, without any link to you. We also delete your customer record at Stripe, if you have one (Stripe keeps the invoices because the law requires it). Technical traces may remain for a short time in the logs and in the backups, as described above.

8. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, delete or receive a portable copy of your data, and to restrict or object to its processing. Where we rely on consent you can withdraw it at any time. Write to swishai23x@gmail.com: we reply within one month. You can also complain to the Italian supervisory authority (Garante per la protezione dei dati personali) or to the authority where you live.

9. Automated decisions

The model counts shots in your video, and the correction check described above can, by itself, pause corrections and leaderboard participation for 30 days. Neither has legal or similarly significant effects on you. You can always ask a person to review it.

10. Age

To create an account you must be at least 14, or the higher age your country sets for consenting to online services (for example 15 in France and 16 in Germany). That is why we ask for your date of birth when you sign up. If you are 14 or 15, your profile stays private, only the followers you accept can see your photo and you do not appear in the world leaderboards; everything else works the same. These features switch on by themselves on your 16th birthday, but your profile stays private until you decide otherwise. If you think a younger child has an account, tell us and we will remove it.

11. Changes

If we change this notice in a way that matters, we tell you in the app or by email before the change applies.
