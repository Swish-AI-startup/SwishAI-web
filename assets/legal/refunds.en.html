This policy covers the SwishAI Pro subscription and is part of the terms of service. Pro codes are free and are not covered by it.

Cancelling

You can cancel at any time from the plans page, with no fee and no reason needed. Cancelling stops the next renewal: you keep Pro until the end of the period already paid and are not charged again. When Pro ends you return to Free without losing any data. You can also resume a cancelled subscription before the period ends.

Cancelling is not a refund: the period already paid runs to its end.

Your right of withdrawal

As a consumer in the European Union you normally have 14 days to withdraw from an online contract without giving a reason.

Pro is a digital service that starts as soon as the payment goes through. Before paying, you ask for it to start immediately and acknowledge that, for the part you have used, you lose the right of withdrawal. The law requires this step. In practice:

  • You subscribed and have not used Pro since (no analysis beyond the Free limits, no clip without the logo, no session beyond the latest five opened): ask within 14 days and we refund the full amount.
  • You subscribed and have used Pro: ask within 14 days and we refund the part of the month you have not used. If the use was minimal we will usually refund everything.
  • A renewal you did not want: tell us within 14 days of the charge and we refund it, if you have not used Pro in the new period.

To withdraw, a clear message to swishai23x@gmail.com is enough, for example: "I withdraw from my SwishAI Pro subscription", with the email of your account.

When we always refund

  • You were charged twice for the same period.
  • You paid but Pro was never activated on your account.
  • A problem on our side made Pro unusable for a significant part of the period: we refund that part.
  • We discontinue a paid feature during the period.

What we do not refund

  • Periods already used, beyond the cases above.
  • Disappointment with the accuracy of the counts: they are estimates (see the terms), and you can try the app for free first.
  • A device too slow for long videos, or a video format your browser cannot read: you can check this on the Free plan before subscribing.

None of this limits the rights the law gives you when a service is not as described.

How to ask

Write to swishai23x@gmail.com from your account's email and tell us what you would like refunded. No form is needed. We reply within 7 days. Refunds go back to the payment method you used, through Stripe, within 14 days of our decision; your bank may take a few more days to show them.

Chargebacks

If something looks wrong, please write to us before asking your bank to reverse the payment: we can almost always solve it faster.
