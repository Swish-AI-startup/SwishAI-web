This policy covers the SwishAI Pro subscription. It is part of the terms of service.

Cancelling

You can cancel at any time from your profile, with no fee and no explanation needed. Cancelling stops the next renewal. You keep Pro until the end of the period you have already paid for, and you are not charged again after that.

Cancelling is not the same as a refund. By default the period you have already paid for runs to its end rather than being refunded.

Your right of withdrawal, and how it works for a subscription

As a consumer in the EU you normally have 14 days to withdraw from a distance contract without giving a reason.

SwishAI Pro is a digital service that starts immediately: as soon as your payment goes through, the higher video limit and the watermark switch are available to you. At checkout you are asked to confirm that you want the service to start right away and that you understand you lose the right of withdrawal once it has been fully performed. That is required by law, and it is the reason for the confirmation step.

In practice:

  • You subscribed and have not processed a video since. Ask us within 14 days and we refund the full amount.
  • You subscribed and have used Pro. The right of withdrawal no longer covers the part you have used. Ask us within 14 days and we refund proportionally for what is left, unless the use was trivial, in which case we will normally just refund you in full.
  • A renewal you did not intend. Tell us within 14 days of the charge and we refund it, provided you have not used Pro in that new period.

When we refund regardless

  • You were charged twice for the same period.
  • You paid but Pro was never activated on your account.
  • A fault on our side made Pro unusable for a meaningful part of the period. We refund that part.
  • We discontinue a paid feature mid-period.

What we do not refund

  • Periods that have already been used, beyond the withdrawal case above.
  • Dissatisfaction with the accuracy of the counts. The app is free to use, its limits are published, and the terms are explicit that counts are estimates from a detection model. Try it on the free plan before subscribing.
  • Your device being too slow to process long videos, or a browser without the hardware acceleration the app warns about. The app tells you before you buy.
  • Videos in a format your browser cannot decode. Check on the free plan first.

How to ask

Email swishai23x@gmail.com from the address on your account and say what you would like refunded. You do not need a form or a particular wording. We reply within 7 days.

Approved refunds go back to the payment method you used, through Stripe. Your bank usually takes a further 5 to 10 days to show it.

Chargebacks

If something looks wrong, please contact us before asking your bank to reverse the payment. A chargeback costs us a fee and locks the payment for weeks, and we can almost always sort it out faster directly.
