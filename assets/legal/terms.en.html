1. What SwishAI does

You give SwishAI a video of someone shooting at a basket. The app detects the ball and the basket, counts shot attempts and baskets made, computes an accuracy percentage, and produces an annotated copy of your video.

The analysis runs on your own device. Your video is not uploaded to us and we never receive a copy of it. See the privacy notice for what does reach our servers.

2. Accuracy

Counts are produced by a machine-learning detection model. They are estimates, not measurements, and they will sometimes be wrong in both directions: a genuine basket can go uncounted and a near miss can be counted. Accuracy depends heavily on lighting, camera position, how much of the basket and the shooter is visible, and how many people and balls are in frame.

SwishAI is designed for a fixed camera filming one basket. It is intended for personal training feedback and entertainment. Do not rely on it where an incorrect count has consequences, such as official scorekeeping, competition results, selection decisions or anything with a financial outcome.
