1. What SwishAI does

You choose a video of someone shooting at a basket. The app finds the ball and the basket, counts shots and baskets, calculates the percentage and shows the result on a player over your original video. You can correct what it counted, save the result in your diary, make a short clip and, if you want, share your results on a public profile and in the leaderboards.

The analysis runs on your device. Your video is not uploaded to us and we never receive a copy of it.

2. Counts are estimates

Counts come from a machine-learning model. They are estimates, not measurements, and can be wrong in both directions. They depend on light, camera position, how visible the basket is and how many people and balls are in the frame. SwishAI is made for a steady camera filming one basket, for personal training and fun. Do not rely on it where a wrong count has consequences, such as official scoring, selections or anything involving money.
